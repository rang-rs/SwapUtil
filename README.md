# SwapUtil

Tracks GP received and given across two game modes, grouped by rates and the partners present in your friends chat.

Enter your group in the sidebar, including yourself, with one RSN per line. Select a mode for each account, then choose a rate direction to edit. Save settings updates only the fields you changed. Settings and records are shared between clients on the same computer.

Customer trades are divided equally among the participating people. Trades with configured group members are recorded separately and excluded from splits. Coins and platinum tokens count as GP; other items are not valued.

Mark settled after making the transfers shown. This starts a new settlement period and preserves the history. Export saves a JSON copy of the records. Rates are entered manually; there is no Discord or cross-computer sync.
